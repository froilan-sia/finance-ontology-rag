# Medium Article Ideas

Article concepts that will come out of this project. Each one is written when the corresponding phase is complete.

---

## Tier 1 — Write these

### 1. Why Your RAG Pipeline Needs an Ontology
**Hook:** Vector search finds similar text. It doesn't understand what the text means.
**Content:** The limitation of pure vector retrieval; what ontology adds; a working example from the finance corpus.
**Timing:** After Phase 1 (weeks 2–3)

### 2. From PDFs to Knowledge Graphs: Building an Ontology for Financial Documents
**Hook:** My RAG pipeline could find the right PDF. It couldn't tell me which company the revenue figure belonged to.
**Content:** Competency questions; class design; Protégé walkthrough; SPARQL queries.
**Timing:** After Phase 3 (week 8)

### 3. The Ontology Skill Gap in Data Architecture
**Hook:** DAMA-DMBOK2 mentions ontology 47 times. It never teaches you how to build one.
**Content:** The gap between frameworks and practice; why AI-era architects need this skill; a learning path.
**Timing:** After Phase 2 (week 5)
